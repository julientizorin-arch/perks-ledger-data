# Perks Ledger — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 14, 2026_

Perks Ledger helps you track credit card statement credits and benefits. It is built so that your information stays on your device.

**What the app stores.** The cards you add, the credits you mark as used, your spending profile and your settings are saved only on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

**No bank connections or accounts.** The app never asks for bank logins, card numbers or account numbers, and you do not need an account to use it.

**Statement import.** If you import a CSV export from your card issuer, the file is read on your phone. Individual transactions are discarded immediately; only yearly totals per spending category are kept, on your device.

**Network use.** The app downloads a public catalog of card terms from GitHub (this repository) to keep card information up to date. That request contains no information about you. Like any web request, GitHub may log basic technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

**Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent to a server to deliver them.

**Analytics and tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

**Deleting your data.** Use Settings → Erase all my data in the app, or delete the app.

**Children.** The app is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes.** Updates to this policy will be posted here with a new date.

**Contact.** Questions can be sent through the TestFlight feedback option in the app.
